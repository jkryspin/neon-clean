### The bug behind today's deaths (important for anyone touching the mob code)

The server sends mob **move** packets only to players who do *not* control those mobs. A client alone on a map controls
all of them; its own moves go out **encrypted** (verified: outgoing bytes are uniform, unlike the weakly obfuscated
incoming traffic, so they can't be decoded). The network mob list therefore froze every mob at its spawn point. The jibs
walked to empty spawn spots and swung at nothing (0 kills in 8 minutes), while the real Orange Mushrooms wandered
over and hit them. "Safe platform" resting failed the same way: the bot couldn't see mobs arriving.

Fix: when the network's mob positions are stale (no move for over 1.5 s), the stream also scans the hitbox rects in
memory. These follow every entity but don't say which is which. They are filtered to rects standing on the map file's
floors, minus the player and other players. Each stale network mob (keeping its oid and template) is then given the
nearest rect and keeps it from tick to tick: cost = dx + 2·dy, 120 px while tracked, and a mob held 2 s if its rect
drops out. Unclaimed rects (NPCs, signs) are dropped. A stream started **inside** a map has no network mob list
(the server lists mobs only on entry), so on a map with spawns the rects are reported as **anonymous mobs** (id none).

Verified live on jib02 in 1020: the reported mobs moved with the screen, the bot fought the Orange Mushroom and Red
Snail standing next to it, and "a mob came to the safe platform → rest in town" fired correctly. Tests: stream 68,
loader 177, all passing.
